Privacy Policy

Your adventure happens on your iPhone. Here is what stays there, what optional services receive, and how to reach us.

Effective 18 September 2026 · Applies to Gut Rush and this website

Service availability: Gut Rush is currently in TestFlight, with live advertising and purchases disabled in distributed build 1.0 (7). This policy also describes the configured optional services for the planned monetized release. Availability depends on your app version; these disclosures do not mean that disabled services process data in your build. All stages and difficulty modes remain playable without buying.

1. Who is responsible?

Gut Rush is developed and operated by Tomás Ruiz López (“we”, “us”). For questions about privacy or requests about personal information we hold, email truizlop@gmail.com. We are responsible for the personal information we process to provide the game, this website and support. Apple, GitHub and other providers also have their own responsibilities for the services they operate.

2. What the game stores

Gut Rush stores your stage progress, scores, stars, achievements, selected difficulty, audio preferences and haptic settings locally on your device. These records make progression and preferences work. We do not operate a Gut Rush account system or a server that receives your ordinary gameplay save.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location or health records. Its digestive-system theme does not involve collecting health information. We do not add a separate analytics SDK or sell personal information.

Deleting the app removes its local app data; an existing device backup may retain a copy or restore it later. Local saves are separate from any achievements or scores already sent to Game Center. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

3. Apple services and beta testing

Game Center

When you connect to Game Center, Apple authenticates your player account. The app can display your Game Center name and submit your total score and earned achievements. Leaderboards and your Apple settings determine what others can see. You can play without connecting; the app saves your progress locally. See Apple’s Game Center privacy information for Apple’s handling of player information and controls.

TestFlight and diagnostics

Apple processes information associated with beta invitations, installations, crashes and usage through TestFlight, and may make beta-testing diagnostics available to us. If you send beta feedback, we receive the feedback and any screenshots or contact information included. Apple’s diagnostic sharing also depends on your device settings. See TestFlight & Privacy. Please avoid including personal or sensitive information in screenshots.

4. Optional advertising

There are no live ads in the current distributed build. When enabled in a release, Gut Rush requests non-personalized interstitial ads through Google AdMob with child-directed treatment and under-age consent treatment. Ads may appear after a completed or failed run, never during active play. Verified Remove Ads access suppresses these interstitials. We do not request Apple’s advertising tracking permission or use personalized advertising. Ad privacy choices appear in Settings when Google’s consent system requires them.

Non-personalized does not mean that no data is processed. With advertising enabled, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location, app or device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and crash, performance and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy. We will update this policy to reflect the actual configuration before enabling live advertising and obtain any permissions required for that use.

5. Optional purchases

Remove Ads purchases are not available in the current distributed build. In a purchase-enabled release, Apple handles payment, and RevenueCat validates transactions and maintains access. That processing includes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase receipts and transaction identifiers, product and subscription status, and technical information such as the app version, device type and operating system. An anonymous identifier is still a persistent identifier; it does not make transaction records anonymous in every context.

We do not receive your complete payment-card details. The integration does not intentionally send RevenueCat your name or email address and disables its automatic device-identifier collection option. Purchase records are used to provide and restore access, prevent fraud, support purchases and produce aggregate purchase analytics when the service is enabled. We do not connect purchase records to Game Center. Purchases do not gate ordinary gameplay. See RevenueCat’s privacy information. The purchase screen shows the available plans and Apple’s localized prices before confirmation.

6. This website and support

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use tracking pixels, or include third-party video embeds. Its fonts, illustrations and gameplay videos are served with the site. The website does not store your browsing choices in local storage.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Our email provider processes those messages to deliver and store correspondence. We use them to answer questions, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy requests. Please send only the information needed for your request. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

7. Purposes, retention and international processing

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process support correspondence as necessary to respond to your request or provide the service, and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and securing the game. We may retain relevant records to comply with legal obligations or resolve a dispute. Where consent is required for an optional activity, we will ask for it and provide a way to withdraw it.

Local progress remains until you remove the app’s data, subject to your device backup settings. We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and related follow-up or legal obligations. Service providers apply their own retention rules to the records they control. You can ask us about, or request deletion of, information we hold about you.

Apple, GitHub, our email provider and any later activated service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their notices explain their locations and safeguards. For transfers we are responsible for, we use the safeguards required by applicable law, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual protections. Contact us for information about a transfer concerning your data.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use the game without connecting to Game Center, control Apple diagnostic sharing through device settings, and contact us about information you have sent. For information controlled by Apple or GitHub, their privacy tools may be the appropriate way to make a request.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive portable information, and withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. These rights can be subject to legal conditions. Email truizlop@gmail.com; we may ask for enough information to verify the request without collecting unnecessary data. For an overview of EU rights, see the European Commission’s information for individuals.

9. Children and families

Gut Rush is designed with children and teens in mind. It has no in-game chat, user profiles or user-uploaded content, and does not ask players for their real name, age or contact details. Parents and guardians can manage Game Center, purchases and other Apple features using their device and family settings.

We do not intentionally ask children to provide personal information to us. A parent or guardian should handle support messages for a child. If you believe a child has sent personal information that should not be held, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Distributed build 1.0 (7) has advertising and purchases disabled. In an enabled release, the advertising integration applies child-directed and under-age treatment to all players, and does not request advertising tracking permission. Apple’s family and Ask to Buy controls govern eligible purchase approvals.

10. Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes and provide additional notice in the app or through the service when required. Material changes to data handling, including activation of advertising or purchases, will be described before they take effect.

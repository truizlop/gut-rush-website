Good to know

Where can I get Gut Rush?

Gut Rush is currently in TestFlight testing and has not had a public App Store release. If you already have an invitation, install the game using Apple’s TestFlight app. You can contact us with questions about testing; this website does not automatically register you or guarantee an invitation.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Ordinary gameplay and local progress work offline. Game Center, beta installation and any future purchase or restore features require a connection.

How do I change the difficulty or sound?

Choose Classic, Rush, Frenzy or Inferno on the home screen. Music and sound effects have separate controls in Settings and the pause menu.

Why are Remove Ads options unavailable?

Live ads and purchases are disabled in the current TestFlight build. You do not need to buy anything to play its stages. If purchases become available, the app will show Apple’s localized prices before you confirm.

What happens to my progress if I delete the app?

Progress is saved locally, so deleting the app may remove it. A device backup may restore it, but Game Center is not a full backup of your local save.

How can I make a privacy request?

Email truizlop@gmail.com. Tell us what information or interaction your request concerns. See the Privacy Policy for more about the app, website and your choices.
